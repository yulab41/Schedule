```powershell
rg -n -C 2 '<pitfall-id>' .agents/skills/schedule-project-guardrails/references/known-pitfalls.md
```

| `icon-multipart-flattened`         | People, multi-layer, or primary/secondary icon is flattened into one uncontrollable image                     | Compare normalized part count and per-part hashes; keep the catalog group/part structure on both platforms. | `icon-semantic-reuse`              | Top user, bottom profile, people, department, calendar, filter, or locate uses the wrong shape/context        | Audit the semantic-context-state matrix and make the business meaning explicit instead of reusing a convenient alias. | `icon-context-drift`               | Size, stroke, color, optical offset, or transform origin differs by page/platform                             | Read context values from the catalog and generated adapters; reject page-private hard-coded action context.
